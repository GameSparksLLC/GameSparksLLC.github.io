Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GameSparks handles information when you play our mobile games. The information a game uses depends on its features and on the choices you make in that game.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies across GameSparks mobile games and related services. Features and data handled can vary between games; if a game does not offer a feature, the related practices below do not apply. This policy describes our shared information practices across those games.

For this policy, “GameSparks,” “we,” or “us” means GameSparks LLC. A store, sign-in provider, or other third-party service may also process information under its own privacy terms.

Information a game may handle

  • Profile and game data: a guest or linked account identifier, display name, settings, inventory, progress, and transactions needed to provide and protect the game.
  • Social content: messages, player or friend activity, reports, blocks, and moderation records when you use those features. Messages and display names can be visible to other players within the feature.
  • Purchase information: product, transaction, and receipt details needed to validate a purchase and grant its contents. Payment card details are handled by the app store, not by GameSparks.
  • Contact information: information you choose to provide through the contact form, including your name, email address, topic, and message. Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect the form from automated spam, and Formspree processes the message so it can be delivered to GameSparks.
  • Analytics and app quality: Where analytics is used, it collects general gameplay and feature-use events, coarse performance measurements, and standard app, device, and usage information. Analytics do not collect direct player identifiers such as your name, email address, display name, or GameSparks account ID. An analytics service may use a pseudonymous app-instance identifier that is not linked to your GameSparks account. We do not ask for separate in-game approval for this general analytics information.
  • Crash diagnostics: Where crash diagnostics are used, services may process the app version, device and operating system details, and crash or error information to help identify and fix problems.
  • Advertising data: a game that shows ads may share data with its advertising provider to serve, measure, or limit ads, subject to applicable requirements and the options shown in that game.
  • Technical information: information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot online features may be processed by the game platform and service providers. The exact fields depend on the feature and provider.

We do not ask for device permissions at launch as a general game practice. A feature that needs a permission should explain it when you choose that feature.

Why information is used

Depending on the game and feature, information is used to provide gameplay and cloud saves, show your selected display name, connect social features, validate purchases, deliver requested support, moderate player content, prevent abuse and fraud, and diagnose service problems.

Where analytics is used, it helps us understand feature use and app quality.

When information is shared

We share information only as needed to provide a feature, protect the service, or meet a legal obligation. Depending on the game, service providers may include Google Firebase for authentication, cloud data, or functions; Google Play or Apple for store transactions and platform sign-in; and an advertising provider such as Google AdMob when that game includes ads. Providers process information under their own terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

We may disclose information when necessary to respond to valid legal process, protect players or the service, investigate suspected abuse, or enforce our Terms.

Your choices and requests

You can play without linking a third-party sign-in where the game supports guest play. If available, linking Google or Apple in the game's profile can help sync or restore your progress. Linking is optional and the provider handles its own sign-in information.

Use the game's settings for the privacy choices it provides. Use profile or account settings to rename, unlink, restore, or request deletion where those controls are provided. For a data or deletion request, contact gamesparksllc@gmail.com and include the game name and enough information for us to locate the relevant profile. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Deleting a profile may remove its cloud saves, social content, and game progress. Some transaction, security, or moderation records may need to be retained for a limited time to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent abuse. A game will explain its deletion flow and any material consequences before you confirm.

How long information is kept

We keep information for as long as needed to operate the feature, maintain account and purchase records, address reports or disputes, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. Retention varies by data type and game. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted or de-identified where practical.

Children and families

Age ratings and audience guidance can differ by game and store. Check the relevant store listing and age rating before a child uses a game. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information can contact us to request review or deletion.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Keep linked account credentials private and contact us if you believe your game profile has been accessed without permission.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as games and services change. The current version is posted on this page.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: gamesparksllc@gmail.com.
